# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the GeeFlow application for mobile devices (Android, iOS) and desktop devices (Windows, macOS, Linux), operated by Krzysztof Dróbek ("Service Provider").

## 1. Overview and Core Principle

GeeFlow is designed with privacy in mind. The Application operates locally on your device. **We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or usage tracking data to external servers.** Registration or creation of an account is not required to use the Application.

The Application is open-source. The core principle of zero data collection can be independently verified by reviewing the source code repository.

## 2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

The Application requests specific device permissions strictly necessary to communicate with supported coffee equipment and provide core functionality:

* **Bluetooth (Bluetooth Scan & Connect):** Used exclusively to discover, pair, and communicate with coffee machines, scales, and accessories via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). The Application never uses Bluetooth signals to track or determine your physical location.
* **Location (Android 11 and older):** Older versions of Android require location permissions to scan for Bluetooth Low Energy devices. This is an operating system requirement for BLE discovery; the Application does not read your GPS coordinates, determine, or track your physical location.
* **Camera:** The Application requests access to your device camera solely for scanning QR codes (e.g., for pairing supported devices or importing configuration profiles). Camera frames are processed locally in real time on your device; no photos, videos, or image streams are ever saved, stored, or transmitted over any network.

## 3. Local Data Storage and Operating System Backups

All data created or managed within the Application — including user profiles, brewing recipes, temperature and pressure profiles, extraction history, and shot logs — is stored **locally on your device** (in local application storage). The Service Provider does not operate any cloud servers, and your data is never transmitted to or synchronized with the Service Provider.

* **System-Level Cloud Backups (Android / iOS):** Depending on your device settings, local application data may be included in your personal operating system backup (such as Google One / Android Backup or Apple iCloud Backup). These backups are managed, encrypted, and hosted entirely by the respective platform operators (Google or Apple) under your personal account. The Service Provider has no access to these backup files. You can disable backup for this Application at any time in your device's system settings.

You have full control over your local data. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Application's storage or by uninstalling the Application.

## 4. In-App Purchases and Tipping

If you choose to support development through the voluntary tip feature, all payment transactions are processed directly by the respective platform operator (Google Play via Google Payments, or Apple App Store via Apple In-App Purchase). The Service Provider does not collect, receive, or store any of your financial, credit card, or billing information.

## 5. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

The Application contains **no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no user tracking frameworks**.

Anonymized technical diagnostics, installation statistics, and crash reports may be gathered directly by operating system providers and app distribution platforms (such as Google Play or Apple App Store) in accordance with your device-level diagnostic settings and the platform operator's privacy policy.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 16 years of age. Since the Application does not collect personal data through normal use, children's data is not at risk when using the Application.

## 7. Contact and Data Requests (GDPR)

Because the Application does not transmit or store personal data on any server, there is no remote user data to retrieve or delete. However, if you contact the Service Provider directly via email (e.g., for support or inquiries), any information you voluntarily disclose will only be used to respond to your communication. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data shared voluntarily via email communication.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, you can reach out via email at drobek.krzysztof@gmail.com.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updates will be published with a revised effective date in the repository and application distribution channels.
